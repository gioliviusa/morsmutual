## 🧠 The First AI‑Powered Leads Finder

**MorsMutal** is a sleek, single‑page web application designed to capture high‑intent leads from insurance agencies looking to buy exclusive, verified prospects. Built with a coastal Ft. Lauderdale aesthetic, it positions the service as the **first AI‑powered insurance leads finder** in Broward County.

The page combines persuasive copy, social proof, a streamlined multi‑step lead form, and subtle animations to maximize conversions on mobile devices.

---

## 🔥 Features

- 🤖 **AI‑Powered Messaging** – Hero badge, announcement bar, and trust strips emphasise proprietary AI verification.
- 📱 **Mobile‑First UI/UX** – Optimized viewport, large touch targets, floating CTA, and vertical rhythm designed for smartphones.
- 🎨 **Coastal Brand Aesthetic** – Deep ocean blues, warm coral accents, and sand tones reflect Ft. Lauderdale's vibe.
- 📈 **Conversion‑Rate Optimised** – Hero stats (2,400+ leads, 94% contact rate), customer testimonial, and a frictionless lead form.
- 🔒 **Trust Signals** – “SOC2 Compliant” badge, lock icon, and 150+ agencies mentioned.
- ⚡ **Smooth Interactions** – Scroll‑reveal animations, floating mobile CTA, shimmering announcement bar, and form loading states.
- 🧩 **Single File** – All HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript in one clean, well‑commented file – zero dependencies.
